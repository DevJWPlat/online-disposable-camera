A web-based disposable camera for one event, with the theme, details, number of shots and gallery reveal time we’ve agreed. How the camera works and its limits

The camera runs in your guests’ phone browsers and uploads each photo as it’s taken, so it needs mobile signal or Wi-Fi. Photo quality depends on each guest’s phone and the lighting. A photo that fails to upload isn’t taken off a guest’s roll, so they can try again. I can’t guarantee that every guest will be able to use the camera or that every photo will upload.
