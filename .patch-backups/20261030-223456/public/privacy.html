Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

The short version: I only collect what I need to run your event camera and reply to you. Guest photos are private to the event organiser, stored securely, and deleted when your storage period ends. I never sell your data or use your photos to advertise without asking first.

Who I am

Online Disposable Camera is run by Jonny Whittle, a sole trader based in the UK. For anything about your data, email hello@onlinedisposablecamera.co.uk.

What I collect and why

  • Enquiries. Your name, email address and the event details you send through the contact form, so I can reply and set up your camera.
  • Bookings. Your contact details and what you ordered, so I can deliver your camera and keep business records. Payments are handled by Stripe, so I never see or store your card details.
  • Organiser accounts. The username or email and password you use to log in to your gallery. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Guest photos. The photos guests take with your event camera, plus the time each one was taken. Guests don’t need to give a name or create an account.
  • Device information. The camera saves a random code on each guest’s phone so it can count their shots and stop the roll being reset. This code doesn’t identify who the guest is.

The legal basis

I use your details to provide the service you’ve asked for (performing a contract), to reply to your enquiry before you book, and to keep the records the law requires. If I ever want to use your photos or event as an example on this website, I’ll ask first and only go ahead with your permission.

Guest photos

Photos taken at an event belong to that event. Only the organiser can see them, through their private login, until they choose to switch on a guest gallery link. They can switch it off again at any time. Organisers are responsible for letting their guests know the camera is in use and for how they share the photos afterwards.

If you’re a guest and want a photo of you removed, ask the event organiser, or email me and I’ll help.

Who else handles your data

I use a small number of trusted services to run the website and cameras:

  • Cloudflare, to host the website and cameras and to store photos and event data.
  • Resend, to deliver enquiry emails to me.
  • Stripe, to take payments securely.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome, which supply the website’s fonts and icons. Your browser connects to them to load these, which shares your IP address with them.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, they use the legal safeguards UK data protection law requires.

How long I keep things

  • Photos are deleted when your storage period ends: three months after the event unless you’ve arranged longer. Download anything you want to keep before then.
  • Enquiries that don’t become bookings are deleted within 12 months.
  • Booking and payment records are kept for six years, because the law requires it for tax.

Cookies and storage

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The event cameras store a small code on guests’ phones to count their shots, and organisers get a login cookie so they stay signed in to their gallery. Both are essential for the service to work.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, object to how I use it, or ask me to stop using it. Email me and I’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. I’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
